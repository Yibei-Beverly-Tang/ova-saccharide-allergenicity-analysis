# Public-release boundary

This repository is intentionally separated from private research materials.

Safe to publish:

- general-purpose analysis code;
- the attributed public UniProt OVA sequence;
- publication-reported summary values with DOI/PMID provenance;
- empty templates and reports generated only from those public records.

Do not publish:

- CV or résumé files and personal contact details;
- manuscript drafts, submission plans or reviewer correspondence;
- unpublished measurements, derived tables, figures or statistics;
- laboratory notebooks, private sample identifiers or proprietary protocols.

`data/private/`, `data/raw/`, common office-document formats and generated
outputs are ignored by Git as an additional guardrail.
